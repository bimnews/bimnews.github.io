v0029	What BIM News is, and what it is not
v0030	<b class="si">It is</b> a collection of information that aims to spread BIM culture across the construction world, and also the curator's personal blog: a small library to read and learn.
v0031	<b class="no">It is not</b> a social network to moderate, nor a place to post opinions: for discussion, we meet later on <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/marco-scigliuolo/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">LinkedIn ↗</a>.
v0032	<b>The site's mission:</b> to make BIM culture known to the whole world. <b>The curator's personal mission:</b> to spread it in Italy, a country deeply attached to its traditions, so that BIM is not just a «certificate» for public tenders but a tool deeply tied to the design method. We can't work miracles, but we try to do things properly. We give it a go.
v0033	BIM around the world, one week at a time
v0034	This is the complete review: every week, BIM news arrives from all over the world, is summarised in Italian and placed on the map, down to the region or state where it happens. Rules and mandates, public procurement, projects, standards and technologies.
v0035	Our criteria: how we choose the news, the sources and the languages
v0036	<b>The underlying principle is inclusiveness.</b> This site exists so that everyone adopts BIM: not only large clients and big engineering firms, but also the small practice, the local builder, the mountain municipality, the student. That is why the news is written in plain Italian, acronyms are explained, open formats and free resources are preferred, and every country that takes a step forward, however small, finds its place on the map.
v0037	The map is global by choice: BIM does not belong to one country. The curator's wish is that every organisation, anywhere in the world, can use this technology to build better, and so to improve the lives of those who live, study and work in buildings.
v0038	How we choose the news
v0039	There is a huge amount of BIM news: every day the trade press publishes updates on products, courses and company case studies. This review does not collect them all: it is a selection of what can genuinely interest designers and people working in the sector.
v0040	<b>Rules and mandates</b>: laws, decrees and deadlines for public procurement
v0041	<b>Standards</b>: ISO 19650, UNI 11337, IFC and the other open formats
v0042	<b>National strategies and procurement</b>: government BIM plans, major projects and public works
v0043	<b>Software and artificial intelligence</b>: only the updates that change the way we work
v0044	<b>Key events</b>: trade fairs, conferences and industry awards
v0045	When interest is equal, we prefer the news that helps someone get started: a free guide, a mandate explained, a first pilot project, a country starting from scratch.
v0046	Which sources, in order of reliability
v0047	<b>Institutional sources</b>, always accepted: governments, ministries, official gazettes, standards bodies (ISO, CEN, UNI, DIN and their national equivalents), national buildingSMART chapters, public contracting authorities, universities and development banks. Cities, regions and provinces count only when they make a concrete contribution to BIM: a mandate, a regulation, a model-based building permit, a programme, a major project.
v0048	<b>Reputable press</b>: trade publications with an editorial team, major national newspapers, news agencies.
v0049	<b>Large companies</b>, only for facts of real weight, such as a major project delivered in BIM; never to present a product.
v0050	Always excluded: personal blogs, websites of individual practices or consultants, resellers, local news, social media posts and sites without author or date. Every news item is checked by opening its source. The archive starts in 1992, the year the expression “Building Information Model” first appears.
v0051	The languages we search the news in
v0052	Each item is searched for in the language of the country where it originates, not only in English.
v0053	<b>Under review</b>: national languages of countries where we expect BIM activity. They stay on the list if they bring valid sources; otherwise they are removed.
v0054	How we chose them is explained in the <a href="#faq">frequently asked questions</a>.
v0055	Every item links to its main source, for those who want to read it in full. When a date or a detail is uncertain, a note says so.
v0056	Explore the news by place, topic and period with the map, the filters and the archive; the BIM & AI tab collects those dedicated to artificial intelligence. In the left-hand column you will also find upcoming events and webinars.
v0057	When the power of artificial intelligence meets BIM
v0058	A BIM model is a large, ordered archive of information: geometry, materials, building services, schedules and costs. Artificial intelligence can read that data and put it to work: it proposes solutions, finds errors and clashes before construction, takes care of repetitive tasks and answers questions in natural language.
v0059	Together they can really make a difference in construction: more consistent designs, safer sites, buildings that use less energy and are easier to manage, for those who build them and those who live in them. Decisions, however, remain with the people who design and sign.
v0060	Here you will find the news in which artificial intelligence applied to BIM is the main topic, with the same map, filters and archive as BIM News.

v0096	How we choose the articles
v0097	We collect scientific articles on BIM published in peer-reviewed journals, the same ones you find on Google Scholar or Academia.edu, searching for them in the open metadata archives of publications (Crossref). We only publish open-access articles under the Creative Commons BY licence: it is the licence that allows the abstract to be reproduced and translated, crediting authors and source.
