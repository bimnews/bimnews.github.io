v0393	A mandate to use BIM does not mean a mandate to be certified. Almost everywhere certification is voluntary: it serves to show a client, through the judgement of a third party, that you can do what you claim. Here is how it works in the countries we have checked, with the rule that governs it and the source.
v0394	People
v0395	The skills of a role are certified, for example BIM Manager or BIM Coordinator: an exam and maintenance over the years. Bodies that certify people are accredited under ISO/IEC 17024.
v0396	Organisations
v0397	It is certified that a practice or a company manages information as required by ISO 19650: an audit of processes and projects, repeated every year.
v0398	Hong Kong
v0399	For public works above 30 million Hong Kong dollars, the appointed party's BIM team must be led by a BIM Manager certified by the Construction Industry Council (CIC). For tenders from 1 January 2024, BIM Coordinators must also be certified (at least half at full level).
