v1475	<span class="ob-k">○</span> Not yet <small>11</small>
v1476	National plans since 2015 (PTNB, Plan BIM 2022).
v1477	<b>Why not yet?</b> The procurement code allows contracting authorities to ask for BIM but does not oblige them, and generalisation is slow; the push could come from the European revision.
v1478	National strategy and roadmap (2024); the first technical guideline (2026).
v1479	<b>Why not yet?</b> There is no threshold above which BIM is mandatory. For the Technical Chamber the key issue is the public administration, which must be able to request, check and use models; resistance to change, BIM seen as a cost and legal uncertainty also weigh.
v1480	Government roadmap (2022) and national technical guide (2023).
v1481	<b>Why not yet?</b> For now the guide applies only to pilot projects; for other public projects it is a recommendation. The criteria for a mandate are postponed to the next phase of the roadmap.
v1482	Large public clients require BIM in their contracts (Trafikverket since 2015).
v1483	<b>Why not yet?</b> There is no national legal mandate: each client decides.
v1484	'Comply or explain' requirement for open standards: IFC (2011), NLCS and COINS (2018).
v1485	<b>Why not yet?</b> The requirement covers the formats used to exchange data, not the use of BIM itself.
v1486	Government strategy (2021) with pilot projects.
v1487	<b>Why not yet?</b> The source does not explain it.
v1488	National strategy approved in 2026.
v1489	<b>Why not yet?</b> The strategy has just started; the source does not set a mandate.
v1490	Roadmap for introducing BIM in public procurement (2020).
v1491	GSA federal programme (2003), NBIMS national standard (since 2007), National BIM Program (2022).
v1492	<b>Why not yet?</b> There is no general federal mandate: individual agencies and clients ask for BIM.
v1493	National strategies for public works (2019).
v1494	<b>Why not yet?</b> The sources do not explain it.
v1495	BIM requirements of the state real estate agencies (Senaatti since 2007, RKAS since 2018).
v1496	<b>Why not yet?</b> These are rules of the public client, not a national law.
