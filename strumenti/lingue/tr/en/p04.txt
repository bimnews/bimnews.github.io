v0230	<b>Attribute or parameter?</b> An attribute is a piece of information attached to an element of the drawing, like the layer or colour of a line: it describes it but does not govern it. A parameter, on the other hand, governs the object: change the value and the shape changes, and with it everything that depends on it.

v0250	The «dimensions» say which information is added to the model beyond geometry. Before 3D there is the two-dimensional CAD drawing (2D). Up to 5D there is agreement everywhere; from 6D the definitions change from country to country, and 8D, 9D and 10D are proposals still under discussion, not standards. That is why in a specification it is better to state which uses and which information are needed, not just a label.
