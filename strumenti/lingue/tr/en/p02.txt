v0134	What is BIM News?
v0135	A weekly review of BIM (Building Information Modeling, the method of designing and managing buildings and infrastructure with a digital model made of objects that carry their own information). It collects news from all over the world, summarises it in Italian, places it on the map and always includes a link to the source. Alongside the news there is a guide for beginners, translated university research, videos, an agenda and manuals.
v0136	Do I need to register or pay?
v0137	No. The site is free and requires no registration.
v0138	The news
v0139	Where does the news come from?
v0140	From three kinds of sources. <b>Institutional sources</b>: governments, ministries, official gazettes, standards bodies, national buildingSMART chapters, contracting authorities, universities, development banks; cities, regions and provinces only when they make a concrete contribution, such as a mandate, a regulation or a major project. <b>Reputable press</b>: trade publications with an editorial team, major national newspapers, news agencies. <b>Large companies</b>, only for facts of real weight and never for advertising. Personal blogs, websites of individual practices, resellers, local news and social media are excluded.
v0141	How do you decide what to publish?
v0142	We publish a fact when it changes something for those who design or build in that country: national strategies and mandates, adopted standards, major public works in BIM, first pilot projects, key events. The underlying principle is inclusiveness: when interest is equal, we choose the news that helps someone get started. Details are in the «How we choose the news» box in <a href="#bim-news">BIM News</a>.
v0143	Why are some countries still empty on the map?
v0144	Because BIM culture has not yet spread throughout the world. The curator would very much like this to change in the future: that is precisely the site's mission, to spread BIM culture everywhere. Meanwhile the search continues every week, in the language of each country, so that every step forward finds its place on the map.
v0145	What do the numbers on the map mean?
v0146	How many news items there are per continent, country or region. Tap a continent, then a country and a region to see the news from that place.
v0147	Why do some items carry the «Archive» label?
v0148	They are items we collected more than seven days after they were published, to complete the history of a country or a standard.
v0149	Why is there no news before 2015?
v0150	2015 is the year the first part of UNI 11337, the Italian series of BIM standards, was published. We use it as a common starting point for the whole world.

v0153	The research languages
v0154	Which languages do you search the news in?
v0155	We search for each item in the language of the country where it originates, not only in English. Below you will find the languages we search in and those we are reviewing.
v0156	They are the national languages of countries where we expect BIM activity. We search them one by one: if after serious research we find no suitable sources, the language leaves the list; if we find them, it moves to the list above.
v0157	How did you choose these languages?
v0158	Around 7,100–7,200 languages are spoken in the world: that is the order of magnitude given by Ethnologue, the reference catalogue. Only a small share has digital content on technical topics. To measure this we used a public, verifiable indicator: the editions of Wikipedia. Wikipedia has 348 active language editions; the article on artificial intelligence exists in 185, the one on BIM in 38. The languages with an article on BIM (excluding Esperanto, which has no reference country) are the starting point of the research. To these we add, under review, the national languages of the countries where we expect activity. Data collected in November 2026.
